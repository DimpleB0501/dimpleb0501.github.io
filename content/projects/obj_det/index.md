<p align="justify">
At AitoeLabs, I worked with a team of engineers to build efficient video
analytics solutions for internal security. For ATM security, we developed
deep learning models and algorithmic solutions operating on live video streams.
</p>

<p align="justify">
I was part of the object detection and tracking group, working on algorithms
for detecting and tracking people in live video streams.
</p>

<p align="justify">
Object tracking assigns unique IDs to detected objects and enables their
behavior to be monitored over time. We developed algorithms to identify
potentially suspicious situations, such as groups exceeding the permitted
number of people inside an ATM or individuals loitering for a prolonged
duration. Detected events were used to alert security personnel or the
responsible company.
</p>
